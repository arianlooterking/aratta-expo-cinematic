Generated with the built-in OpenAI image workflow on 2026-07-13. The supplied
English Aratta Expo logo was used as the identity reference. The approved final
booth pair is stage 06; stages 01-05 follow the same booth through construction.

1. Clean exhibition hall with a restrained branded project sign.
2. Cyan technical floor layout, base rails, and flight cases.

4. Completed frame with glass, lighting, and reception installation.
5. Brand-ready booth during the final professional inspection.
6. Completed booth with an Iranian professional reception and meeting team.

## Shared visual prompt

Photorealistic full-frame architectural and editorial exhibition photography;
locked straight-on camera; realistic blackened steel, bronze, glass, stone, floor
wear, fabric, skin, anatomy, and lighting; professional Iranian women in modern
black business clothing and head hijabs, not chador; one exact unobstructed Aratta
Expo logo; no headline, slogan, invented lettering, watermark, extra logo, CGI
plastic finish, or fantasy architecture.

The PNG files in this folder are retained as generation masters. The production
site uses quality-focused 4K WebP exports under `public/hero-v6/`.
